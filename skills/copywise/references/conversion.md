# Conversion writing

Start with the purchase or sign-up decision. Identify the person making it, what they want to accomplish, what the offer actually includes, and the first step available. Use a sentence that would stop making sense if pasted onto a competitor selling a different product.

## Build a claim from a feature

1. Find a documented capability. Translate it into a task the person can picture.
2. Show how it works with a concrete object, output, or preview. Add evidence only when it belongs to this offer. Preserve the source, denominator, time period, and qualification of any material number.
3. Match the action to the next interaction. A link to a calendar can say “Choose a demo time”. A form that asks sales to follow up can say “Request a demo”.
4. Put the condition that changes the decision beside the decision. Include the billing period, amount due, renewal terms, or access limits when applicable and known. Use comparable units for plan differences.
5. Remove any sentence that repeats the heading with a different adjective. If proof is missing, demonstrate the mechanism or omit the claim. Never manufacture a countdown, testimonial, guarantee, savings figure, or scarcity.

## Worked landing section

Brief: a meal planner combines ingredients from selected dinners into one grocery list. Visitors can browse recipes before joining. The brief provides no time-savings data.

| Element | Copy | Why it earns the space |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heading | Pick your dinners. Get one grocery list. | Names the choice and the output. |
| Supporting text | Making tacos and soup? Ingredients from both recipes go on the same list. | Shows the mechanism using a recognizable weeknight task. |
| Primary action | Browse dinners | Predicts the public recipe gallery. |

If the next step changes to account registration, update the action to “Create an account”. If the example sits next to a working recipe picker that already demonstrates the mechanism, try removing the body line.

## Pricing and checkout

Suppose a plan costs $48 today and renews annually at $48. A compact offer can say “$48 per year” and “Pay $48 today. Renews annually at $48.” A monthly equivalent can be supplemental. Keep the amount charged and its cadence in view where the person commits.

For each CTA, read it beside its alternative. “Subscribe” and “Keep free plan” describe the choices when a free plan really exists. Preserve the person’s ability to decline without having to endorse a negative statement about themselves. When amount, cadence, cancellation, or the next route is unknown, use verified facts in exploratory copy and identify the missing detail before finalizing purchase copy.

## Evidence pass

A real mechanism can carry a B2B headline. If partners choose jobs by trade and service area, “Choose jobs by trade and ZIP code” gives contractors a concrete reason to look. Add a volume claim only if it represents these leads, in this market, for the stated period. A platform-wide total cannot establish what one contractor will receive.
