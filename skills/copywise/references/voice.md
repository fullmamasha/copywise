# Voice and product categories

Voice governs vocabulary and rhythm across the product. Tone responds to the person’s situation. Use these profiles as defaults when the project has no editorial direction. Teams can request any profile by name in ordinary language.

## Four voice profiles

One fact across all four examples: a user saved a set of three places for a weekend trip. The action opens the saved list. Assume the count is available and the plural is localized.

| Profile | Voice direction | Saved state | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Playful | Allow one small moment of delight after the task succeeds. Give it a concrete hook. | Three places saved. Looking like a good weekend. | View saved places |
| Modern and youthful | Use spoken rhythm, short clauses, and familiar nouns. Let brevity do the work. Slang needs evidence from this audience. | Weekend picks saved | View saved places |
| Business | Use stable terminology and lead with the work completed. Include owner, scope, or status when it affects the next decision. | 3 places saved to your weekend list | View saved places |
| Financial and formal | Lead with the confirmed event. Use restrained wording and consistent terms for amounts, dates, and parties. | Weekend list updated | View saved places |

The same action label can serve every voice. Personality often belongs in a title or a moment of recognition. In a dense table, a familiar noun usually does more for the reader.

## Address in Russian

In Russian, choosing a voice also means choosing «ты» or «вы». Follow the product’s established form. When there is none, playful and modern profiles usually take «ты», while business, financial, health, and public-service products take «вы». Keep one form across the whole product, including errors, payment, and legal screens. Buttons in the infinitive, such as «Открыть список», work with either form.

| Profile | Address | Saved state | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Playful | ты | Три места в списке. Намечаются хорошие выходные | Открыть список |
| Modern and youthful | ты | Подборка на выходные сохранена | Открыть список |
| Business | вы | 3 места добавлены в список на выходные | Открыть список |
| Financial and formal | вы | Список на выходные обновлён | Открыть список |

Each line is built around the list rather than the reader. «Ты сохранил три места» would assume the reader’s gender, which the interface usually does not know. The rules for this and other Russian grammar live in [Russian interface writing](russian-product-writing.md). Ukrainian chooses between «ти» and «ви» the same way, and German, French, Spanish, and other European languages have their own defaults for address. See [Ukrainian interface writing](ukrainian-product-writing.md) and [European languages](european-languages.md).

## How the voices sound in their own setting

- **Playful, cooking.** After someone chooses their final dinner, “That’s dinner sorted” can sit above a summary of the week. The action “View grocery list” stays literal if it opens that list.
- **Modern and youthful, music.** “What’s on repeat?” can introduce a selection of favorite tracks. “Pick 3 songs” earns a second line when three selections are required.
- **Business, team software.** “12 invoices need approval” gives the finance team a work queue. “Review invoices” opens it. When the queue is empty, “All invoices reviewed” requires evidence of review. “No invoices awaiting approval” also works for a queue that started empty.
- **Financial and formal, banking.** “€80 sent to Alex’s bank” describes a confirmed handoff. Add an estimated arrival only when the transfer record provides one. “View transfer details” is enough for the action.

## Choose by product and moment

| Product category | Starting voice | Where it comes through | Moment to handle plainly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Games and learning | Playful | Mark a real achievement. “Seven days of piano practice” gives the celebration a subject. | Missed days, failed uploads, purchases |
| Social, music, and creator tools | Modern and youthful | Use the creator’s object. “Pick a cover” works for a playlist when the UI calls the artwork a cover. | Audience visibility, reporting, public sharing |
| Cooking, travel, and lifestyle | Modern and youthful or playful | Ask an everyday question. “What’s for dinner?” can introduce a recipe choice. | Allergens, bookings, cancellation terms |
| Retail and marketplaces | Modern and youthful or business | Use recognizable item, delivery, and seller language. | Final price, recurring charges, returns |
| B2B software and developer tools | Business | Match the team’s nouns and precise scopes. “Invite to workspace” distinguishes membership from project access. | Role changes, publishing, bulk edits |
| Banking, insurance, and payments | Financial and formal | Identify the amount, currency, recipient, and confirmed stage where relevant. | Every commitment, decline, or uncertain result |
| Health, public services, and sensitive accounts | Plain business with warmth | Explain the task and who sees the information, when known. | Results, eligibility, identity, account recovery |

## Change the tone inside one flow

In a playful music app, finishing a first recording could earn “Your first take is in.” Choosing an audience needs a direct question such as “Who can hear this track?” A failed publish with a confirmed saved draft can say “Your track didn’t publish. Your draft is saved.” Keep the word *track* across the flow.

If brand and state pull in different directions, keep familiar product terms and remove the joke. Warmth can come from telling someone their work is safe when the product confirms it. Never infer data retention for the sake of reassurance.
