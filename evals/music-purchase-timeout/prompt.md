Use Copywise. We're adding a one-off purchase to our music app, which has a playful brand: an $18 "Studio Pack" of samples. Checkout has two screens. On the first, the button opens an order summary. On the summary screen, the button submits the purchase and charges the card. If the payment request times out, we don't know whether the charge went through, but the order page can fetch the current status. Write the copy for both buttons, the summary screen heading and supporting line, and the timeout state (title, body, action).
